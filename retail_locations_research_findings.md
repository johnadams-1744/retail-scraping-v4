## Detailed Findings

### 1. The Perfume Club - perfumeclub.com
**FALSE** — Online perfume retailer. The Perfumes Club (theperfumesclub.com) is Bahrain-based; lists Sesam Street 323b, 4010 Norway as contact but appears to be primarily e-commerce. No confirmed physical storefront open to public.

### 2. 7th Heaven Chocolate - 7thheavenchocolate.com
**FALSE** — No brand-owned retail. Products available at Stop & Shop grocery stores (NY, NJ, New England). Store locator finds third-party retailers only.

### 3. Coconut Pops - coconutpops.com
**FALSE** — E-commerce only. Company closing operations; running 60% off closing sale. No brick-and-mortar locations.

### 4. Acadian Sturgeon and Caviar - acadian-sturgeon-and-caviar-inc.myshopify.com
**TRUE** — Physical retail at **248 Douglas Avenue, Saint John, New Brunswick, Canada**. Hatchery/processing at 30 Carters Wharf Road, Carters Point, NB E5S 1S5 (open for visits).
